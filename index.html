A practical buyer guide

Homebuyer Mistakes Checklist

Buying a home is a major decision. Use this checklist to spot common mistakes early, ask better questions, and move forward with greater confidence.

Buy
smarter
01

Focusing only on the advertised price

Remember to account for the down payment, taxes, loan fees, insurance, association dues, utilities, moving costs, repairs, and ongoing maintenance.

02

Buying before checking financial readiness

Do not assume you can afford a property based only on a sample monthly payment. Review your income, debts, savings, emergency fund, and financing eligibility first.

03

Choosing a location without testing daily life

Check commute times, transportation, schools, healthcare, shops, safety, drainage, flood exposure, and nearby developments at different times of day.

04

Skipping a careful property inspection

Look beyond finishes and staging. Ask about structural condition, leaks, plumbing, electrical systems, drainage, parking, storage, repairs, and what is included.

05

Signing before understanding the documents

Read the reservation, contract, fees, deadlines, refund terms, inclusions, restrictions, and turnover conditions. Ask for clarification before signing or paying.

06

Rushing because of pressure or urgency

A limited-time offer should not replace due diligence. Compare suitable options, verify important details, and take time to make a decision that fits your goals.

Before you make an offer

Confirm your total budget, financing plan, property condition, documents, contract terms, and long-term suitability. If you are unsure, ask for professional guidance before committing.

This guide is for general information only. Financing, legal, tax, and document requirements may vary, so confirm details with the relevant professional or institution.
